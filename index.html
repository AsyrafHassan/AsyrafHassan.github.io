Six themes from SCST1133, in the order they build on each other. Open a theme to see what I learned, the evidence behind it, and how it connects to the rest of the course.

How the themes fit together: a simplified data pipeline

  1. Acquire collect data from sources
  2. Store keep it organised and safe
  3. Process clean and transform
  4. Serve deliver to people and systems

Governance, data quality, privacy and ethics apply at every stage, not only at the end.
